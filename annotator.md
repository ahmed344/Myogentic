# Annotator guidance

Apply this guidance alongside the shared instructions in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## Annotation principles

- Annotate visible morphology, not a definitive patient diagnosis. H&E alone does not provide the full diagnostic context.
- Allow coexisting myopathic, dystrophic, inflammatory, and neurogenic patterns rather than requiring a single exclusive label.
- Support findings, pattern presence, severity or extent where appropriate, overall abnormality, uncertainty, and optional comments or evidence regions.
- Distinguish absent, uncertain, not assessable, and unassessed. Never interpret an unanswered field as absence.
- Keep uncertainty separate from severity. Use "no abnormality identified on this H&E section" rather than claiming the patient is healthy.
- Require an explicit main-pattern choice before completing a review, alongside the richer annotations. Allow no dominant pattern, uncertain, and cannot assess as explicit choices; drafts may remain unassessed.
- Retain each doctor's assessments, revisions, and any adjudicated result separately. Track annotation definitions as they evolve.

## Annotator experience

- Provide responsive whole-slide navigation, zooming, and clear optional overlays.
- Minimize repetitive actions with compact forms, keyboard shortcuts, autosave, progress tracking, and easy resume.
- Make annotations easy to export for training and evaluation while preserving their meaning and provenance.
